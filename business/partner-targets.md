## Partner Integration Roadmap for Finance-Guard

### Target Integrations

| Partner/API                  | Free-Tier Limits                       | Integration Effort | Value-Add (User Job)                                       | Rationale                                                                 |
|------------------------------|---------------------------------------|--------------------|-----------------------------------------------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Plaid**                    | Up to 100 transactions/month          | M                  | Streamlining transaction data retrieval                    | Widely used in financial services; enhances data accuracy and speed.     |
| **Stripe**                   | $0 for up to $1M in transactions     | M                  | Simplifying payment processing and reconciliation           | Popular payment processor with affiliate program; boosts transaction flow.|

| **Tink**                     | Free for up to 100 transactions/month | M                  | Enabling account aggregation and payment initiation         | Strong in European markets; enhances user capabilities and satisfaction.  |

### Summary
The integrations listed above are prioritized based on their potential to enhance the Finance-Guard product's value proposition, focusing on transaction monitoring and issue resolution. Each partner offers unique capabilities that align with the core functions of Finance-Guard, while also providing opportunities for revenue-sharing and affiliate partnerships. The integration efforts range from small to large, allowing for flexibility in implementation based on resource availability.
